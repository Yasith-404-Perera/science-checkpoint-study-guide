# 📚 Cambridge Checkpoint Science — Complete Study Guide

A free, open-source revision guide covering all four strands of the **Cambridge Lower Secondary Checkpoint Science (0893)** syllabus. Built as a single HTML file — no installation, no login, no nonsense. Just open it in your browser and start revising.

---

## 🧪 What's Inside

The guide covers every examinable topic across all four science strands:

| Strand | Topics covered |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **Biology** | Cells & Organisation, Water Transport in Plants, Photosynthesis, Respiration, Genetics & Inheritance, Human Body Systems, Ecosystems & Food Chains, Health & Disease |
| 🟣 **Chemistry** | States of Matter, Atoms, Elements & Compounds, Bonding & Structure, Chemical Reactions & Rates, Acids, Bases & Salts, The Periodic Table, Carbon Chemistry |
| 🔵 **Physics** | Forces & Motion, Energy & Heat Transfer, Waves & Sound, Light & the EM Spectrum, Electricity & Circuits, Magnetism & Electromagnetism |
| 🟠 **Earth & Space** | Structure of the Earth, The Rock Cycle, Earth's Resources, The Atmosphere & Climate Change, The Solar System, Stars, Galaxies & the Universe |

---

## ✨ Features

- **One file, works offline** — download `index.html` and open it in any browser. No internet needed after download.
- **Tabbed strand switcher** — jump between Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Earth & Space with one click. Colour theme updates per strand.
- **Sticky topic navigation** — quickly jump to any topic within a strand without scrolling.
- **Exam tips throughout** — highlights common mistakes, mark-scheme language, and how to structure answers.
- **Comparison tables** — side-by-side breakdowns of easily confused concepts (e.g. aerobic vs anaerobic, series vs parallel circuits).
- **Key equations** — all Physics and Chemistry equations formatted clearly for quick reference.
- **Top 10 facts per strand** — a summary of the highest-frequency exam content to review before the paper.
- **Fully responsive** — works on mobile, tablet, and desktop.

## 🚀 How to Use

### Option 1 — Open directly (easiest)
1. Download [`index.html`](./index.html)
2. Double-click it — it opens in your browser
3. Done. No setup required.

### Option 2 — Clone the repo
```bash
git clone https://github.com/Yasith-404-Perera/science-checkpoint-study-guide.git
cd science-checkpoint-study-guide
open index.html   # Mac
# or
start index.html  # Windows
```

### Option 3 — View it live (GitHub Pages)
If the repo owner has enabled GitHub Pages, you can view it at:

## 📖 Topics in detail

<details>
<summary><strong>🟢 Biology</strong></summary>

- Animal and plant cell components; specialised cells (root hair, red blood cell, sperm, nerve, muscle)
- Diffusion and osmosis
- Levels of organisation (cell → tissue → organ → system → organism)
- Water pathway in plants; xylem vs phloem; transpiration factors
- Mineral salts (nitrates, magnesium, phosphates)
- Photosynthesis equation; limiting factors; starch test method
- Aerobic and anaerobic respiration; fermentation
- Genetics vocabulary (gene, allele, dominant, recessive, genotype, phenotype)
- Punnett squares; sex determination; chromosome numbers
- Digestive system enzymes; circulatory system; excretory system
- Food chains and webs; energy transfer; decomposers; human impacts on ecosystems
- Pathogens (bacteria, viruses, fungi, parasites); immune system; vaccines
- Non-communicable diseases (cardiovascular disease, cancer, Type 2 diabetes)

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>🟣 Chemistry</strong></summary>

- Particle theory; properties of solids, liquids, and gases
- Changes of state; energy changes; diffusion in gases and liquids
- Atomic structure; protons, neutrons, electrons; isotopes; electron shells
- Elements, compounds, and mixtures; separation techniques (filtration, distillation, chromatography, Rf values)
- Ionic bonding (electron transfer); covalent bonding (electron sharing); metallic bonding
- Types of reactions: combustion, oxidation, reduction, thermal decomposition, displacement, precipitation
- Collision theory; factors affecting reaction rates
- Balancing chemical equations
- pH scale; neutralisation; acid + base/carbonate/metal reactions; salt naming
- Gas tests (H₂, O₂, CO₂, Cl₂, NH₃)
- The Periodic Table; groups and periods; Group 1, Group 7, noble gases, transition metals
- Reactivity series
- Fossil fuels; complete vs incomplete combustion; the carbon cycle; greenhouse effect

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>🔵 Physics</strong></summary>

- Speed, force, weight, pressure, and moment equations
- Newton's three laws of motion
- Friction, drag, and terminal velocity
- Pressure in fluids; upthrust and floating
- Energy stores (kinetic, gravitational potential, elastic potential, chemical, thermal, nuclear, electrical, light, sound)
- Key energy equations (KE, GPE, work done, power, efficiency)
- Conservation of energy; heat transfer by conduction, convection, and radiation
- Wave properties (amplitude, wavelength, frequency, period); wave speed equation (v = fλ)
- Transverse vs longitudinal waves; sound waves; hearing range; ultrasound
- Reflection and refraction; angle of incidence and reflection
- The electromagnetic spectrum (radio to gamma); uses and dangers of each
- Convex and concave lenses
- Ohm's Law (V = IR); power and energy equations; series vs parallel circuits; static electricity
- Magnets; magnetic fields; electromagnetism; electromagnetic induction

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>🟠 Earth & Space</strong></summary>

- Earth's four layers (inner core, outer core, mantle, crust); tectonic plates and convection currents
- Plate boundary types (convergent, divergent, conservative); earthquakes and volcanoes
- Three rock types (igneous, sedimentary, metamorphic); how each forms; identifying features
- Rock cycle processes; types of weathering (physical, chemical, biological)
- Fossil fuels; renewable energy sources and their pros/cons; the water cycle
- Atmosphere composition (N₂, O₂, Ar, CO₂); natural and enhanced greenhouse effect
- Causes and effects of climate change; acid rain
- The solar system; planet order; orbital motion; gravity; day, night and seasons (axial tilt)
- Solar and lunar eclipses
- Life cycle of stars (nebula → main sequence → red giant/supergiant → white dwarf/neutron star/black hole)
- Nuclear fusion in stars; how elements are made
- Galaxies; the Universe; the Big Bang theory; red shift as evidence

</details>
